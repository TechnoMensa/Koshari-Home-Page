History

TechnoMensa began as a student project during a game development course at TU Graz in 2023. Creating the prototype for our first game sparked our passion for game development and inspired us to continue working on the project beyond the classroom.

Over time, the project evolved into Koshari Defense, which saw its first demo release on Steam in October 2024. Today, TechnoMensa remains an independent passion project driven by a team of three dedicated game developers.
